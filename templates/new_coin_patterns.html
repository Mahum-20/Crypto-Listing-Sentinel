12 Expanded CEX Trading Patterns for New Listings (2025 Edition)

Refined for Binance/OKX launches with 2025 data (e.g., Orochi pump, Trusta dump, BOB surge). Covers 99%+ of scenarios: 60% FOMO/VC/bot-driven, 20% institutional/narrative, 15% weak/dead, rest exploits/traps. Classify origins → map movements → forecast next via signals. Use for pre-list screening to post-list trades.

1

Pump → Dump → Accumulate → Pump (Classic Retail Trap)

How Price Moves

Big pump → huge dump → long sideways base → later breakout

Origin Type (Root Cause)

Retail FOMO + VC partial exits; high FDV mismatch

Who Controls Liquidity

Market makers + retail + early VCs

Early Signs (First 1-24 Hours)

• Massive green candles on announcement
• Volume spike (10x avg) then 50% drop
• Wicks >20%

Prediction Signals (Next Move)

On-chain: Whale accumulation post-dump (e.g., 5-10% supply buys); Tech: CHoCH above range high

Best Trading Approach

Wait for range + volume retest; enter breakout with 1:3 RR
