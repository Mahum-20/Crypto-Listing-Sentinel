Entry Strategy After Prediction

Your analyzer gives a solid "go up" signal, but dips (e.g., liquidity hunts) are common in new coins. Wait for confirmation before entering—don't chase the prediction blindly. Aim for 2-3 aligning signals from below.

This is your comprehensive strategy to turn predictions into profitable trades. Track 5-10 past predictions to refine—backtest entries.

1. Technical Confirmation (Quick Check)

  • Support Bounce: Enter when price hits a key support (e.g., launch price or 20-30% dip) and bounces with volume spike.
  • RSI Oversold: Buy if RSI (14) <30 (oversold) and starts rising.
  • Price Action Shift: From "Immediate Dump" to "Small Pump" or "Sideways" on your analyzer—enter on breakout above recent high.

2. On-Chain Signals (Deeper Dive)

  • Whale Accumulation: No more "Whale Inflows? Yes" or "Dev Dump % >5%"; watch for holder growth (tools like DexScreener).
  • Liquidity Stabilize: Depth >$100K USD and "Holders Concentrated? No" (less risk of rugs).
  • Bot Activity Drop: "High Bot TX? No" to avoid fake volume.

3. Sentiment & Macro (Final Green Light)

  • Hype Build: Social mentions up 2x+ (Medium/High level) without negativity.
  • Macro Align: "Macro Bullish? Yes" (e.g., BTC stable/up)—avoid entries in red markets.
  • Competitors: Low overlap; if high, wait for unique edge (e.g., better utility score).

Risk Rules (Always)

  • Position Size: 1-2% of portfolio max.
  • Stop-Loss: 10-20% below entry (trail up on gains).
  • Timeframe: Enter within 24-72 hrs post-launch; if no signals, skip.
  • Tools: Use TradingView for charts, Dune Analytics for on-chain, LunarCrush for sentiment.

This strategy turns "okay" predictions into profitable trades. Always DYOR and trade responsibly.
