New Coin Trading Checklist

Complete all steps for 95% risk coverage. Run analyzer after Step 2.

Pre-Launch Fundamentals (Steps 1-3)

Step 1: Tokenomics & Coin Info

Verify FDV (<5x TVL), float (>20%), vesting cliffs (<10% Day 1), supply/inflation.

Tools: TokenUnlocks, CoinGecko

Step 2: Team, Backers & Audits

Score team (1-10), check VCs/celeb backs, full audit status. Run pattern analyzer here.

Tools: Messari, Certik, LinkedIn

Step 3: Narrative & Utility Fit

Does it match 2025 trends (AI/RWA/politics)? Unique edge vs. competitors?

Tools: DefiLlama, whitepaper

On-Chain & Liquidity (Steps 4-5)

Step 4: Holder Distribution & Whale Flows

Top 10 <30%, no clusters; inflows from rotations, no dev dumps (>20%).

Tools: Bubblemaps, Arkham/Lookonchain

Step 5: Liquidity & Order Book Depth

LP >$500k, thick bids (2x asks); flag thin books or fake walls.

Tools: Dexscreener, CEX order books

Technical & Live Analysis (Steps 6-7)

Step 6: Pattern & Structure ID

Match to 12 patterns; spot CHoCH, FVG, OB, liquidity sweeps.

Tools: TradingView, your analyzer

Step 7: Momentum Indicators

RSI (<80 overbought), MACD cross, funding rates (<0.01%), pre-launch DEX vol.

Tools: Coinglass, 1m/5m charts

Market/Sentiment & Execution (Steps 8-10)

Step 8: Social Hype & Sentiment

Mentions >5x avg, authentic KOLs; no paid shills or sentiment crash.

Tools: LunarCrush, X/Telegram search

Step 9: Macro & External Catalysts

BTC >$80k? Altseason? No bear signals (Fear >70) or chain issues.

Tools: Fear & Greed, CoinMarketCap

Step 10: Risk Management Setup

Position <2% portfolio, SL at -20% (range low), TP at +50%; hedge if perps.

Tools: Journal/Excel for RR calc
