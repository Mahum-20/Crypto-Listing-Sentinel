Analyzer Guide: How to Gather Data for Beginners

Step-by-step procedures to collect values for each analyzer field. Use the checklist page first, then input here. Tools are free/basic; takes 10-20min per coin. Focus on new CEX/DEX launches (e.g., Binance/OKX, Solana Pump.fun).

1. Fundamentals (40% Weight)

Assess project viability pre-list. Score subjectively where needed (1=poor, 10=excellent).

FDV (USD)

Market cap at full dilution (circulating supply x price).

  • Step 1: Go to CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap, search coin name/symbol.
  • Step 2: Under "Tokenomics" or "Markets", find "Fully Diluted Valuation" (FDV).
  • Step 3: Enter exact number (e.g., 150000000 for $150M). If pre-launch, calculate: Total Supply x Pre-List Price from DEX.
Tools: CoinGecko.com, CoinMarketCap.com

Vesting Cliff %

% of tokens unlocked on Day 1 (VC/team dumps trigger Pattern 2).

  • Step 1: Visit TokenUnlocks.app, search coin symbol.
  • Step 2: View "Unlock Schedule" chart; note % unlocked at launch date.
  • Step 3: If no data, check whitepaper (project site) for "Token Vesting" section; estimate % for private/seed rounds.
Tools: TokenUnlocks.app, Project whitepaper

Team Score (1-10)

Subjective rating of team's credibility/experience.

  • Step 1: Search team names on LinkedIn (from project site/Discord).
  • Step 2: Check GitHub for commits (active >50 last month?); past projects (no rugs?).
  • Step 3: Score: 10=ex-Google/VC-backed; 1=anonymous/no track record. Average team members.
Tools: LinkedIn.com, GitHub.com, RugDoc.io

Utility Score (1-10)

Rating of real-world use case vs. hype.

  • Step 1: Read whitepaper/exec summary on project site.
  • Step 2: Check Messari.io for TVL/metrics; partnerships listed?
  • Step 3: Score: 10=proven product/TVL> $10M; 1=pure meme/no roadmap.
Tools: Messari.io, Project website

Audit Status

Security review level (none=high rug risk).

  • Step 1: Search "coin name audit" on Google or project site/docs.
  • Step 2: Check Certik/PeckShield reports (full=complete scan; partial=basic).
  • Step 3: Select: Full (passed audit), Partial (in-progress), None (no mention).
Tools: Certik.org, PeckShield.com
Beginner Tip: If FDV >$500M + no audit, skip—80% bleed risk (Pattern 3).

2. On-Chain (30% Weight)

Track wallets/liquidity post-launch (first 1-2h). Use blockchain explorers.

Holders Concentrated?

Yes if top holders control >30% (dump risk).

  • Step 1: Go to Etherscan.io (ETH) or Solscan.io (Solana), search contract address.
  • Step 2: Click "Holders" tab; sum top 10 % holdings.
  • Step 3: Yes if >30% or 3+ wallets >5% each (clusters).
Tools: Etherscan.io, Solscan.io, Bubblemaps.io (visual clusters)

Liquidity Depth (USD)

Total buy/sell order value near price (thin < $100k = volatile).

  • Step 1: On Dexscreener.com or CEX (Binance), search pair.
  • Step 2: View "Depth" chart (24h); sum bids/asks within 2% price.
  • Step 3: Enter total USD (e.g., 750000). Pre-list: Check LP pool size.
Tools: Dexscreener.com, TradingView order book

Whale Inflows?

Yes if large wallets (>1M USD) buying early.

  • Step 1: Set alerts on Whale-Alert.io or Lookonchain.com for coin symbol.
  • Step 2: Check last 1h txns: Inflows > outflows?
  • Step 3: Yes if >$1M net buy from labeled whales (Arkham).
Tools: Whale-Alert.io, ArkhamIntelligence.com

Dev Dump %

% supply sold by dev wallet in first hour.

  • Step 1: Find dev wallet on Etherscan/Solscan (from launch tx or whitepaper).
  • Step 2: View "Token Transfers" tab; sum sells since launch.
  • Step 3: % = (sold / initial hold) x 100 (e.g., 5 for low risk).
Tools: Etherscan.io, Solscan.io

High Bot TX?

Yes if >50% txns from bots (fake volume, Pattern 8).

  • Step 1: On Dune.com, query "coin txns" dashboard.
  • Step 2: Filter new wallets (<1 day old); count % of total txns.
  • Step 3: Yes if bundles (5+ txns/block) or >50% bot-like (repeat small buys).
Tools: Dune.com (free queries), Dexscreener txns tab
Beginner Tip: Start with free explorers; upgrade to Nansen ($100/mo) for pro whale tags.

3. Technical (20% Weight)

Use charts post-open (1-5m timeframes). Beginner: Follow TradingView tutorials.

Initial Price Action

First 5-10min move type.

  • Step 1: Open TradingView.com, search coin pair (e.g., COINUSDT).
  • Step 2: Switch to 1m chart; zoom to launch time.
  • Step 3: Select: Massive Pump (>20% up), Immediate Dump (>10% down), Sideways (<5% range), Small Pump (5-20% up).
Tools: TradingView.com

RSI (14-period)

Momentum oscillator (0-100; >70 overbought=dump signal).

  • Step 1: On TradingView, add "RSI" indicator (default 14 periods).
  • Step 2: View current value on 5m chart.
  • Step 3: Enter exact number (e.g., 75). If no chart, approx from price action.
Tools: TradingView.com (free indicators)

Negative Funding?

Yes if perp funding rate <0 (shorts pay longs, bearish).

  • Step 1: Go to Coinglass.com, search coin futures.
  • Step 2: Check "Funding Rate" (8h rate); current value.
  • Step 3: Yes if <0%; No if >0% or N/A (spot-only).
Tools: Coinglass.com, Binance futures page
Beginner Tip: Watch YouTube "TradingView RSI tutorial" (5min); ignore if no perps.

4. Market/Sentiment (10% Weight)

Gauge external hype; quick social scans.

Social Hype Level

Overall buzz (high=Pattern 1 risk).

  • Step 1: Search coin on X.com/Twitter (last 24h).
  • Step 2: Count posts: >100=high, 20-100=medium, <20=low.
  • Step 3: Check tone (positive?); select based on volume/engagement.
Tools: X.com advanced search

Social Mentions Multiplier

Avg daily mentions vs. baseline (5x=rotation signal).

  • Step 1: Use LunarCrush.com, search coin.
  • Step 2: View "Social Mentions" 24h vs. 7d avg.
  • Step 3: Multiplier = today / avg (e.g., 6 for 6x spike).
Tools: LunarCrush.com (free tier)

Macro Bullish?

Yes if market favors alts (BTC stable, fear low).

  • Step 1: Check alternative.me/crypto-fear-greed-index.
  • Step 2: Fear/Greed <50? BTC >$80k? Altseason (ETH/BTC >0.05)?
  • Step 3: Yes if 2+ green signals; No otherwise.
Tools: Alternative.me, CoinMarketCap.com

Competitor Overlap?

Yes if saturated niche (e.g., 10th AI token).

  • Step 1: Search "similar to [coin]" on CoinGecko categories.
  • Step 2: Count rivals in same tag (e.g., DeFi, Memes).
  • Step 3: Yes if >5 active competitors; No for unique.
Tools: CoinGecko.com categories
Beginner Tip: Set Google Alerts for coin name; cross-check with checklist Step 8.
